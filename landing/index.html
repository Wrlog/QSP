Quantitative systems pharmacology models

QSP models join a drug's pharmacokinetics to its pharmacodynamics through the biology of the disease: pathways, cells and feedbacks, down to a clinical endpoint. Each model here was rebuilt from its original publication as an mrgsolve model and runs in your browser; nothing to install. The first load takes about half a minute while R starts up in the page.

Warfarin and the coagulation cascade

54 speciesPT / INRVitamin K

The humoral coagulation network with warfarin PK and the vitamin K cycle. Clotting factors fall at their own turnover rates, and the prothrombin time of a simulated plasma sample gives the INR.

Open the app
Wajima T, Isbister GK, Duffull SB. Clin Pharmacol Ther 2009;86:290-298.

How it works

Every model is a single mrgsolve model file. Run locally with mrgsolve installed, the apps simulate through mrgsolve. In the browser the same file is translated to R and solved with a stiff Rosenbrock solver, and the test suite checks the two engines against each other, and each model against its published results, before every deployment. Source: github.com/Wrlog/QSP. For physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PK-only) models, see the PBPK models.

For research and teaching only. Nothing here is validated for clinical use or should guide the treatment of a patient.
